# Concept: Key Opening Prices (Time-Based PO3) — Nq Cam

**Source:** "How to CORRECTLY Use Key Opening Prices (Time-Based PO3)" — Nq Cam — https://youtu.be/guQ2ctgXYCg
**Plugs into:** Step 4 (PO3 / time) of `01-ifvg-model.md`

> **Core rule: opening prices give LOCATION, not BIAS.**
> Your higher-timeframe draw on liquidity (DOL) sets the bias. The opens only tell you *where* in time and price to look for the entry.

---

## The four opens to mark every day (New York time)

| Open | What it frames |
|---|---|
| **00:00 — True day (midnight) open** | The whole daily candle / daily profile |
| **08:30 open** | NY morning delivery. Most important on **red-folder news days at 8:30** (CPI, NFP, etc.) |
| **09:30 — Cash open** | The cash session |
| **10:00 open** | Starts a new 4H candle; another NY-morning reference |

Each open starts its own mini-PO3: **open → add liquidity → manipulate → distribute**. Several of these play out inside one day.

---

## How to use them

**Premium / discount in time (paired with price):**
- **Bullish bias** → want price to manipulate **BELOW** the relevant open(s) — a *time discount* — ideally also in a *price discount* of the dealing range, into a bullish PDA, sweeping liquidity. Then look for the long entry.
- **Bearish bias** → want price to manipulate **ABOVE** the open(s) — a *time premium* — plus price premium, into a bearish PDA, sweeping liquidity. Then look for the short.
- **Below/above an open alone means nothing.** It still needs: manipulation into real liquidity or a PDA, **plus confirmation** (e.g. an IFVG) before entry.
- The more opens price is on the "right" side of, the stronger the location (e.g. long when below the 10:00, 9:30 AND 8:30 opens, inside a bullish PDA).

**Reading the daily candle with the midnight open:**
- Midnight open → rally into premium, sweep buy-side → likely the daily **open-high** of a bearish day (O-H-L-C). Expect the low later.
- Midnight open → drop into discount, sweep sell-side → likely the daily **open-low** of a bullish day (O-L-H-C).
- Once the daily candle has **already hit its objective** (e.g. bearish day already took the major sell-side), stop looking for more continuation — expect a **retrace/rebalance toward the close** instead.

**Opens as reference levels during the session:**
- Price often returns to and reacts at these opens. Watch for **bodies closing through** them (intent) vs. just wicks ("wicks do the damage, bodies tell the narrative").
- Combine with other PDAs: FVGs, consequent encroachment (50%) of gaps, OTE zone.

---

## Mistakes the video warns about
- Shorting just because price manipulated above the 9:30 open into a bearish PDA, when price was **already in a deep discount** and the daily bearish objective was **already met** → that short failed; the long from the discount was the better trade.
- Treating an open as a buy/sell signal by itself.

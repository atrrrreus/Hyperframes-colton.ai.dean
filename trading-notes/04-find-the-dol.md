# Concept: How to Find the Correct DOL (ReeseTradesNQ)

**Source:** "How to Always Find the Correct DOL" — ReeseTradesNQ — https://youtu.be/XKk87Ruogrk
**Plugs into:** Step 1 (A+ DOL) of `01-ifvg-model.md`
**Note:** creator claims 80%+ accuracy for picking the DOL — his claim, not verified.

---

## The 4-step process (do in this order, every day)
1. **Identify the possible DOL**
2. **Set your HTF conditions** (if-this-then-that scenarios)
3. **Identify which HTF range you're in** (decides how you use the conditions)
4. **Wait for LTF confirmation** of the condition

---

## Step 1 — Possible DOL (two questions)
**A. Are we delivering FROM buy-side or sell-side liquidity?**
- Just swept sell-side (ideally with an SMT vs ES) → idea = **buy-side** is the target.
- Just swept buy-side → idea = **sell-side**.
- If HTF is too choppy to tell: look at which side price is *trending toward*.

**B. Which FVGs are respected vs. disrespected?**
- Bullish DOL confirmed when: **bullish gaps respected** + **bearish gaps disrespected** (closed through).
- Bearish DOL: the opposite.

> **Key idea:** step A only gives you the *idea*. The DOL is **confirmed by what the imbalances (FVGs) are doing**.

---

## Step 2 — HTF conditions
- A **condition = an HTF FVG (15m, 30m, 1H, 4H, daily)**.
- Every gap both **attacks** one side and **defends** the other.
- Build two scenarios per condition:
  - **Scenario 1:** condition **respected** → expect DOL in its direction.
  - **Scenario 2:** condition **disrespected** (closed through) → expect the opposite DOL.
- Usually the newest gap on the most recent leg (scenario-dependent each day).
- Whether it's respected or not is decided on the **lower timeframe** (Step 4).

---

## Step 3 — Which HTF range are we in?

**High-resistance (HR) high/low:** swing that **swept liquidity** + **traded into an HTF FVG** + **led to distribution** (left aligned PDAs behind).
**Low-resistance (LR) high/low:** failure swings — no sweep, no HTF gap. Easy to take.

| Range | How to trade it | Condition to use |
|---|---|---|
| **HR vs LR** (easiest) | Trade **away from HR, toward LR** | The 15m+ FVG on the **recent expansion leg** out of the HR side. Respected → target the LR side. Disrespected → expect a return to the HR side (no flip yet). |
| **LR vs LR** | (a) Wait for a **sweep** of one side **into its condition**, then target the opposite LR side. (b) Or wait for an **expansion out of the range**: condition closed through + **new 15m+ FVG** opens → trade the **15m continuation** to the next HTF DOL. | An HTF gap **beyond each side** of the range (e.g. 4H above and 4H below). |
| **HR vs HR** (hardest) | Mark **equilibrium (50%)**. Find a **bearish gap in premium** and a **bullish gap in discount**. **Bearish day:** premium bearish gap respected + discount bullish gap disrespected → range low. **Bullish day:** discount bullish gap respected + premium bearish gap disrespected → range high. | The two gaps either side of EQ. (Gap only needs to be *partly* in discount/premium.) |

> ❌ **In LR vs LR — don't trade while price is still inside the range.**

---

## Step 4 — LTF confirmation
- An HTF condition is confirmed as **respected** when the **opposing LTF bias is disrespected** → an **LTF IFVG** (or sometimes a **CISD**).
- Example (bullish): price trades into a 15m bullish gap and leaves a **30-second bearish gap** = new LTF condition.
  - 30s gap **holds** → the 15m condition is failing.
  - 30s gap **inverted (closed above)** → 15m respected → **buy-side confirmed as DOL** → trade is valid.
- This is the same IFVG trigger as file 01.

> **Core idea:** PO3 means nothing without context. A wick only counts as **true manipulation** if it reaches a **key level (HTF PDA)** and fits your narrative/DOL.

## OXXC — how every candle forms (1m → yearly, fractal)
| Candle | Sequence | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **Bullish (O-L-H-C)** | Open → manipulate **lower into an HTF PDA** → entry model → expand to buy-side DOL → retrace → close | Long after the low wick forms |
| **Bearish (O-H-L-C)** | Open → manipulate **higher into an HTF PDA** → entry model → expand to sell-side DOL → retrace → close | Short after the high wick forms |

**Time premium / discount:** the candle's **open price** splits it.
- Below the open = **time discount** → best for longs.
- Above the open = **time premium** → best for shorts.
- Best longs: **time discount + price discount** together (and vice-versa for shorts).

---

## True manipulation — how to tell which wick matters
A candle usually prints **both** a top and bottom wick. The one that counts is the one that:
- [ ] **Trades into an HTF PDA** (FVG, BPR, order block, mitigation block…)
- [ ] **Sweeps internal liquidity** (internal high for shorts / low for longs)
- [ ] Ideally comes with an **SMT vs ES**
- [ ] Leaves **low-resistance liquidity** on the other side to target
- [ ] **Agrees with your HTF narrative / DOL**

A wick that hits no PDA just **adds to accumulation** — ignore it.
**ES can provide it:** if NQ's wick hits no PDA but ES sweeps internal liquidity with an SMT, that can still be the true manipulation.

---

## Rules from the examples
1. **Don't BE the manipulation.** If the current PO3 candle (e.g. 15m) **hasn't printed its wick yet** in the direction of your stop, your entry can get swept to form that wick. Wait for the bottom wick (longs) / top wick (shorts) **into an HTF PDA** first.
2. **Stacked PO3s = highest probability.** When the **15m, 30m and 1H** PO3s all show manipulation into a PDA pointing the same way, take the entry (example: 2m IFVG → full TP).
3. **Ignore manipulation that fights the narrative.** E.g. 9:30 sweeps internal sell-side but the target above is a **high-resistance high inside an HTF bearish PDA**, while lots of low-resistance liquidity sits below → skip the long. Wait for the wick **into the HTF PDA that matches your bias** (there: a 5m PDA + internal buy-side sweep) → short on the 1m IFVG.
4. **Re-check at each new PO3 open** (e.g. new 15m) while the bigger one (30m) is still open — a fresh manipulation into a PDA in the same direction = continuation.

# Concept: Stacked PO3 — Nq Cam

**Source:** "Increasing Your Win Rate Using Stacked PO3" — Nq Cam — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QM69ib4Y-30
**Plugs into:** Step 4 (PO3 / time) of `01-ifvg-model.md`. Extends `02-key-opening-prices.md` and `06-oxxc-po3-true-manipulation.md`.

> **Core idea:** PO3 is fractal. Lower-timeframe PO3s (e.g. 9:30, 9:45) **build liquidity**; the **higher-timeframe PO3** (e.g. 10:00 15m/1H) provides the **real manipulation**, then distributes to everything the smaller ones built.

---

## How stacking works
1. LTF candle opens → generates liquidity (no real manipulation yet).
2. Next LTF candle opens → adds more liquidity to the same pool.
3. **HTF candle opens → manipulates into an HTF PDA → distributes** to all the liquidity built in steps 1–2.

Example (bearish): 9:30 moves lower but leaves major sell-side intact → 9:45 adds to the sell-side pool → **10:00 opens, wicks up into a price + time premium inside an HTF PDA** → accelerates down to all the DOLs below. Expect the 10:00 15m / 1H candle to be **open → top wick → distribute → close**.

---

## Which manipulation matters
- ✅ **Real manipulation:** **both NQ and ES sweep liquidity inside an HTF PDA**, and the sweep **meets the current model's objective**.
- ❌ **Not manipulation:** internal highs/lows swept with **no key level** behind them while price is still seeking its objective. These are just higher highs (in a buy model) / lower lows (in a sell model) — ignore them.
- **Use objectives to find the HTF manipulation:** in a buy model, nothing below gets targeted until the **buy-side objective is met** (and an HTF PDA is tapped). Only then expect distribution to the liquidity built on the way up.

---

## Time + price premium / discount
- **Price:** above the dealing range's 50% (EQ) = premium (shorts); below = discount (longs).
- **Time:** relative to the **open of the current HTF candle** — above the open = time premium, below = time discount.
- Best: **both** aligned. **Time alone can be enough** in some scenarios (e.g. not in price premium, but in time premium).

---

## Fade / take rules
- ❌ **Fade** setups where the HTF objective isn't met yet → the HTF PO3 will likely **manipulate your stop first**, then distribute without you.
- ❌ A new HTF open making a higher high **with no HTF PDA there and a major DOL still above** = just generating liquidity for a later PO3. Not a short.
- ✅ In that case a **continuation long** can make sense: price edged a major buy-side DOL, the new open builds liquidity above, then **manipulates lower into an HTF bullish PDA** (which defends the lows and attacks the buy-side) → IFVG long to the DOL above.
- Always check **current DOL + narrative** first. PO3 stacking is a filter, not the whole criteria.

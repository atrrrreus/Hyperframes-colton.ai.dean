# Concept: Navigating LTF Entries in Different HTF Conditions — Nq Cam

**Source:** "Navigating LTF Entries in Different HTF Conditions" — Nq Cam — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vZo5pNebgzI
**Plugs into:** Step 3 (range type) in `04-find-the-dol.md` + attack vs. defense in `11`. Adds a section for all-time-high conditions.

> **Core question in every range:** *What is currently **protected**, and what is currently **low resistance**?*
> Your stop must sit behind what's protected; your target must be the current LR — even if the bigger external levels say otherwise.

---

## HR vs HR (both range extremes are high resistance)
- Both sides swept liquidity inside HTF gaps (e.g. ITL in a 4H bullish FVG, ITH in a 4H bearish FVG). Don't target the externals blindly — **react to internal conditions** (15m / 30m / 1H gaps formed inside the range).
- **Bullish internal gap hit + strongly rejected** → it's protected → target the **nearest unmitigated bearish gap above** (the current LR), even though the range high is HR.
- Then at that bearish gap:
  - **Strongly rejected** → expect the **range low**.
  - **Disrespected** → expect the **range high** + externals.
- Example: weekly bullish FVG tapped → displaced into a 4H bearish FVG (rejected) → tapped a 4H bullish FVG, leaving an unmitigated **1H bearish FVG** → long to that 1H gap (lots of LTF low resistance leading up to it) → 1H gap rejected → range low.

---

## LR vs LR (both extremes are easy liquidity)
- Same idea: mark the internal conditions. E.g. a **1H bullish FVG** inside the range:
  - **Hit + strongly rejected** → the low below it stays protected (nothing is attacking it) → target LR above first.
  - **Disrespected** → short-term bearish to the LR below first.
- ❌ **Don't use an unsponsored, premium LTF gap (e.g. 5m)** that's only defending HTF low resistance — it has no key level behind it and just adds liquidity below.
- ✅ Valid example from the day: a **3m bearish FVG** respected + the bullish side **inverted** → short; a 3m ITH protecting the stop vs. quadruple equal lows → 1H unmitigated gap as target.

> **If you can't tell what the current DOL is → don't trade. Wait for HTF manipulation.** Some days the information never comes — that's fine. Put the ego aside.

---

## All-time highs / strong trend
**Default: trade WITH the trend.** Lots of liquidity below doesn't mean short — only once the HTF actually turns bearish.
- **Longing ATH:** react off **HTF (e.g. 30m) bullish PDAs** that are being strongly rejected, even in premium.
  - Example: **10:30 PO3** opened, added liquidity above, manipulated down into a **30m bullish FVG** → LTF bearish disrespected + HTF bullish rejected → long → full TP to new highs.
  - Still need full criteria. ❌ An **unsponsored 5m** at ATH = pattern trade.
- **Shorting ATH** — only when the HTF shows the turn:
  - [ ] HTF **bearish PDAs being created and respected**
  - [ ] HTF **bullish PDAs being disrespected**
  - [ ] Next HTF bullish PDA / major sell-side identified as the target (e.g. daily bullish FVG below)
  - [ ] ITH swept **inside a 1H bearish PDA** + displacement lower

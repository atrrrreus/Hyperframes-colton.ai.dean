## The rule
**True manipulation = BOTH pairs (NQ and ES) trade into a higher-timeframe FVG.**

**Question the reel answers: when can we VOID that requirement?**
→ When only ONE pair reaches its HTF gap, but the two pairs show one of these three SMTs. The SMT proves the stronger pair didn't need to manipulate — it's already distributing toward the HTF DOL.

---

## The three SMT types that void true manipulation
(Diagrams are drawn bullish: HTF DOL above, 1h FVG below. Flip for bearish.)

### 1. Double SMT
- Pair A **never trades into its 1h FVG** — it holds **higher lows**.
- Pair B **trades into its 1h FVG** and makes a **lower low**.
- Two SMTs at once:
  - **HTF SMT** — one pair is in the gap, the other isn't.
  - **Internal SMT** — the low-timeframe swing lows also disagree (A higher low, B lower low).
- Then both expand to the **HTF DOL**.

### 2. ITL / ITH SMT (intermediate-term low / high)
- Both pairs have an intermediate-term swing low near/inside the 1h FVG.
- Pair A **holds** its ITL (higher low); pair B **sweeps** its ITL (lower low) inside the gap.
- Then both expand to the HTF DOL.

### 3. UM FVG SMT (unmitigated FVG SMT)
- Pair B trades into its 1h FVG; pair A leaves its 1h FVG **unmitigated** (doesn't reach it), making a higher low.
- The disagreement at the gap is the SMT. Price may wick back toward the gaps again, then both expand to the HTF DOL.

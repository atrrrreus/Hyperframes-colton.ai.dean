# Concept: Current DOL > Daily Bias — Nq Cam

**Source:** "How we can identify our CURRENT DOL..." — Nq Cam — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ulurCGAf6JQ
**Plugs into:** Step 1 (DOL) of `01-ifvg-model.md` — read together with `04-find-the-dol.md`

> **Core idea:** Daily bias is overemphasized. As a day trader, the real question is **"What is my CURRENT draw on liquidity, and how will price get to the bigger target?"**
> You can be bullish on the day and still expect — and trade — a move lower first.

---

## Market maker model is fractal
- **Sell side of the curve:** price sweeps internal liquidity and keeps delivering from HTF **bearish** PDAs, moving lower.
- **Transition to buy side:** after major sell-side is taken → displacement higher → price starts **respecting HTF bullish PDAs**.
  - Don't call it buy side until you see that displacement + bullish PDAs being respected + liquidity built above.
- **Buy side of the curve:** price takes back all the liquidity it built on the way down, retracing into bullish PDAs to fuel each leg higher.
- The same pattern repeats on every timeframe.

---

## The "objective met → rebalance" rule
1. **Heavy displacement = price is seeking an objective** (e.g. internal buy-side).
2. Displacement also **leaves imbalances (gaps) behind** = new liquidity.
3. **Once an objective is hit, expect a rebalance** back into the unmitigated PDA it left behind — **even if bigger targets still remain in the same direction.**
4. That rebalance is the **fuel** for the next leg.

Chain to track all day:
**Sell-side objective met → sponsors move higher → buy-side objective met → sponsors move lower → (repeat)**

**Key question every time:** *Which move is currently being sponsored, and where is it going?*

---

## Bullish day bias — what to expect intraday
- Bias bullish (lots of liquidity above) **≠** price goes straight up.
- You **want to see price lower first** — to rebalance and grab liquidity — then continuation higher out of that manipulation.
- If price just **swept internal buy-side inside an HTF bearish PDA** and there are **unmitigated bullish PDAs below**:
  → be **short-term bearish** until that bullish PDA is mitigated, then watch the reaction:
  - **Rejected with displacement** → longs back toward the bigger DOL.
  - **Disrespected (closed through)** → bias can flip bearish.
- (Flip all of this for a bearish day.)

---

## Trade-filtering lessons from his examples
- **Want discount before longs:** don't long until price has reached at least the **discount (below 50%)** of the dealing range that sponsored the move. Shallow pullbacks = "not enough liquidity grabbed".
- **Skip setups with no internal model:** a small 3m IFVG right after a hard dump = "catching a falling knife". Wait for **internal accumulation → manipulation → distribution**, or very obvious displacement.
- **Don't short into deep discount + HTF bullish PDAs** (e.g. inside a 4H order block / 1H bullish BPR that's being respected), even if the LTF short looks clean. Prefer to wait for the long continuation.
- **Don't long into a "wall" of unmitigated bearish gaps** right above you.
- **"Wicks do the damage, bodies tell the narrative"** — judge whether an HTF PDA is respected by candle **bodies**, not wicks.
- An **internal SMT (NQ vs ES)** at a manipulated high/low supports the move being sponsored from there.

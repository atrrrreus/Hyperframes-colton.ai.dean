# Concept: Pattern Trading IFVGs Will Wreck You — Nq Cam

**Source:** "Pattern trading IFVG's will wreck you...here's how to increase your win rate." — Nq Cam — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hJHkpHrH004
**Plugs into:** Final filter before any IFVG entry in `01-ifvg-model.md`. Pulls together `05` (current DOL), `10` (stop hunts), `11` (attack vs defense).

> **Core idea:** an IFVG with no narrative behind it is a **pattern trade**. A V-shaped inversion + an internal sweep is NOT a reason by itself.
> And stop calling every loss a "good loss" — **use your journal to find the repeating reason your losses lose.**

---

## Mindset: no more "good losses"
- If the same type of loss keeps happening, there's a root cause. Look at your data, find the pattern, cut that setup.
- Being complacent about losses = red P&L and no consistent payouts.

## Rules to filter out pattern trades
- [ ] **HTF narrative agrees** with the trade (don't fade a clear HTF buy/sell model before its objective is met)
- [ ] If it's a **coin flip** what price does next → **no trade**
- [ ] **Location:** longs from **discount**, shorts from **premium** of the dealing range — not before
- [ ] The manipulation hit an **HTF PDA** (move is sponsored)
- [ ] **Stop at HR, target at LR** — never the reverse
- [ ] **Target not already mitigated**
- [ ] **No falling knife:** after a huge one-way move, wait for accumulation + stop hunt (see `10`)
- [ ] **Between two HR levels with nothing generating LR** → sit out the day
- [ ] After a **failed delivery** (no displacement, chop) → don't re-enter the same idea
- [ ] Bodies telling the story at the HTF PDA ("wicks do the damage, bodies tell the narrative")

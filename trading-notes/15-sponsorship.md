# Concept: Sponsorship — High-Probability Continuation — Nq Cam

**Source:** "Sponsorship: How to Catch High Probability Continuation/Re-Distribution" — Nq Cam — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VOAL3J_Vobg
**Plugs into:** Step 2 (HTF FVG / sponsored gap) of `01-ifvg-model.md`. Links to OTE displacement ranges (`07`), stop hunts (`10`), attack vs. defense (`11`).
**Note:** he credits these concepts to ICT.

> **Core idea:** after **HTF manipulation**, the whole first displacement leg is **sponsored**. Gaps inside that leg are high-probability places for continuation if you missed the first entry. Gaps created later by smaller moves are **not** sponsored. Taking those is FOMO.

---

## What counts as sponsored
1. **HTF manipulation**, e.g. buy-side swept inside a 4H bearish FVG, so the buy-side objective is met and a major sell-side objective remains.
2. The **initial displacement leg** away from it (swing high → swing low) is sponsored **as a whole**, not just one gap in it.
3. **Any HTF-enough gap inside that leg** (15m, 5m…) is a valid retrace area for continuation **while the major objective is still intact**.
   - If the leg has two gaps (e.g. a lower 5m and a higher 5m/15m), either one is a valid area.

## What is NOT sponsored
- A gap formed **later**, from a move off a smaller (LTF) manipulation. Example: a 5m gap created while running from the 15m continuation entry.
- **Any continuation after the major objective is already met.**
- Taking these = "settling for less" because you missed the real move → **skip and move on**.

---

## Context still decides (when the lower sponsored gap fails)
**Fade the lower sponsored gap when:**
- Price **dumped hard and barely retraced**, so you're in **deep discount** of the range.
- **Major sell-side has already been swept** (lots of the objective already done).
- An **unmitigated higher-TF gap (e.g. 15m) sits at your stop** and in premium. Price will likely manipulate into it first.

→ Wait for the retrace into that **higher sponsored gap in premium**, then take the IFVG. Example: the 1m IFVG off the 5m failed; the 5m IFVG off the 15m worked. The "play of the day" was then a **15-second IFVG** after an internal buy-side sweep into a 5m bearish gap, with low resistance below.

**Take the lower sponsored gap when:**
- **No unmitigated higher-TF gap at the stop.**
- No major liquidity swept yet between you and the objective, so nothing resists.
- Example: 30m-gap manipulation at the open → retrace into the 5m sponsored gap → **30s IFVG** → hit the major sell-side.

> Sponsorship means nothing without narrative. **Check the DOL and context first.**

---

## Quick checklist
- [ ] HTF manipulation happened (liquidity swept inside HTF PDA)
- [ ] Major objective still intact
- [ ] Retrace gap is **inside the initial displacement leg** from that manipulation
- [ ] No unmitigated higher-TF gap at the stop (otherwise wait for it)
- [ ] Not already in deep discount after the objective's liquidity was mostly taken
- [ ] Missed it? → **don't take the FOMO gap from a later LTF leg**

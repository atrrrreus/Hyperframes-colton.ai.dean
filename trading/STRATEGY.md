# IFVG Strategy Playbook — @atrrreus

NQ / ES futures · ICT concepts · IFVG entries.
Built from reel analysis (ZeroTwoTrades, KBTrades, ict.tommy, tbn.nq, lvlup.david, ict.bryce) and my own KBT Challenge trades.
Win rates quoted by creators are their claims — this is a checklist to **backtest and journal**, not a guarantee.

**Core rule: No conviction, no click.** If I doubt it before entry, I don't enter.

---

## Step 1 — Bias / Draw on Liquidity (DOL)

**Higher-timeframe bias — ask two questions** (tbn.nq):
1. Where is price delivering *from*?
2. Which gaps are being respected? Bullish gaps holding + bearish gaps closed through → bullish (and vice versa).

**The three DOL scenarios** (ZeroTwo):

| Scenario | Looks like | Action |
|---|---|---|
| HR vs LR | High resistance one side, low-resistance liquidity (LR) the other | Use the HTF FVG defending the move, target the LR side |
| LR vs LR | Liquidity on both sides | Wait for one side to be taken, then target the opposing LRL using HTF conditions |
| HR vs HR | No clean liquidity | Draw the range 0 / 0.5 / 1. Discount bullish FVG respected + premium bearish FVG disrespected → range high (inverse → range low) |

**Key opens (TDO, 6AM, 9:30 etc.) give location, not bias** (ZeroTwo). Ask:
1. Did the PO3 of that open **manipulate**, or only **build liquidity**?
2. Has price already reached the objective?
- Manipulated but DOL not reached → use the key open as a **continuation reference point** (e.g. EQ of the displacement range / 9:30 open, then displace toward DOL).
- Only built liquidity → **the missing manipulation is the DOL** (e.g. 6AM didn't manipulate up → expect 9:30 to sweep SSL and draw to the 6AM open).

**Unsure about bias → fade the day and journal it.** Better to fade a day than force a trade.

## Step 2 — Model (where am I in the curve?)

**Continuation (preferred):** price is already distributing toward the overall DOL. Enter from a HTF gap (IRL) or a sponsored LTF gap, with **LTF LR leading to the DOL**.

**Reversal / counter-trend (riskier):** only when ALL of these are present (KBTrades, lvlup.david, ict.bryce):
- Price **stalls inside the key level** (not a clean V into it)
- **Structure + liquidity build on both sides of the reversal curve** (LRL generated away from the level)
- A **stop raid / resweep / turtle soup** of the swing into the key level (often a sponsored gap)
- Then CISD + IFVG toward the draws
- Do NOT take the first 1m inversion against a huge displacement (e.g. a 500-pt pump with healthy closes) — that's FOMO.
- If no proper reversal: take **continuation from the unfilled gap**, or wait for structure to form and take continuation the other way. You don't need the exact top/bottom.
- Patience costs some R:R but gives a narrative and a much higher hit rate (ict.bryce "the curve").

**MMXM curve:** if price leaves failure swings on the sell side of the curve, wait for it to transition to the other side before taking the reversal.

**KEY: there must be LTF LR leading up to the draw.** No LR toward the target = targeting high resistance → expect re-manipulation first.

## Step 3 — True manipulation: NQ + ES in sync

- **Both NQ and ES must be at / reacting from a HTF PD array** before entry (they can use different FVGs/timeframes).
- NQ tapped HTF + gave IFVG but ES hasn't tapped → **my stop is the liquidity**. Skip.
- Exception: **double SMT** with ES counts as true manipulation.
- ES reaches its spot but fails to close inside / invert → pairs not in sync → no trade.

---

## Step 4 — Time / PO3

- Every HTF candle: open → manipulation wick → body → accumulation → close. Treat the **HTF open as Time EQ**.
- **Bullish:** after the HTF open, price trades into **time discount**, bottom wick inside a HTF FVG+, then ride distribution.
- **Bearish:** into **time premium**, top wick inside a HTF FVG−.
- **Accumulation usually happens in the last 5 minutes of a HTF candle** (lvlup.david).
- **Never enter an inversion in the last 1–5 minutes before a HTF candle opens/closes.** The new candle makes its own manipulation and raids the stop.
- **Take inversions after a HTF open.**
- "Price was aligned but time was not" = failed trade. Time + price aligned = A+.

## Step 5 — Key level & entry

**Key level** (tbn.nq): a **FVG on 5m or higher** — untouched, or the low resting inside a gap already traded into.

**Which FVG to invert** (ict.tommy):
- Longs: invert a FVG in the **premium** of the manipulation leg. Shorts: in the **discount** of the leg.
- Multiple FVGs in the leg → scale up timeframes, use the **highest-timeframe FVG in the leg** (5m max per tbn.nq) and wait for it to close through.
- **Avoid legs that are too big** — that's a market-structure change, not manipulation; the inversion comes late with bad R:R.

**Stop hunt first** (ZeroTwo): at the HTF FVG, wait for the swing to get **reswept** before entering. SL then sits behind a swept (high-resistance) level. No resweep = catching a falling knife.

**IFVG quality:** inverts quickly with strength, **healthy close within 4–5 candles**. Too slow = skip.
Enter on the close of the inversion candle.

"IFVG doesn't move the market. The context around it does."

---

## Step 6 — Management

- **SL:** below/above the manipulation low/high (the swept level). Never at an obvious unswept level.
- **BE:** only at the **mechanical BE point** (after price breaks the intermediate high/low post-sweep). Never earlier.
- **TP:** bare-minimum target — first LR / DOL: equal highs/lows, 15m highs, session highs, open gaps, in the HTF direction. **1:1 is enough.**
- No second-guessing once in. SL and TP manage the trade.
- Some days the setup won't show. That's normal. Don't force it.

## Pre-trade checklist (score /10 — trade only 10/10)

| # | Check |
|---|---|
| 1 | Bias clear (DOL scenario + gaps respected). Unsure → no trade |
| 2 | Model identified: continuation or reversal |
| 3 | If reversal: stall at level + liquidity both sides of curve + stop raid. Otherwise continuation only |
| 4 | LTF LR leading to the DOL |
| 5 | NQ and ES both at a HTF PD array (or double SMT) |
| 6 | Time aligned: in time discount/premium of current HTF candle, after a HTF open |
| 7 | Not within 5 min of a HTF candle open/close |
| 8 | Key level = 5m+ FVG; inverting the highest-TF FVG in the leg, in premium (longs) / discount (shorts); leg not oversized |
| 9 | Resweep / stop hunt happened before entry |
| 10 | IFVG inverted fast (≤4–5 candles, healthy close) |

Log 8/10 and 9/10 setups as "seen, not taken" to check later whether skipping was right.

**Journal tags:** `Model: Cont/Rev` · `DOL: HR-LR / LR-LR / HR-HR` · `Pairs: Sync / Not / Double SMT` · `Time: Aligned / Not` · `Score: X/10` · `Exit: TP / SL / BE early / Manual` · `Stopped then hit TP? Y/N` · `Emotion pre-entry 1–5`

**Judge a trade by the checklist, not the result** (KBTrades). A win on a 6/10 trade is still a bad trade.

## My recurring mistakes (from KBT Challenge)
- Moving to BE before the mechanical BE point → stopped, then price hits TP (Day 5)
- Entering right before a new HTF candle (PO3 time) (Days 5, 26)
- Trading with doubt / no resweep → wick to SL, then TP (Day 5)
- Trading sessions outside my plan (London, Day 9) and FOMO entries against the move (Days 9, 16)
- Revenge trading across multiple accounts after a loss (Days 10, 16)
